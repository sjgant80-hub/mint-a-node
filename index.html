The problem with renting intelligence

You're sending a repetitive task — triage, classification, extraction, routing — to a giant frontier model, over and over. Three things follow:

✕You pay per token, forever

Every call bills again. A task you run a million times a month is a bill that never stops and only grows.

✕Your data leaves the building

Every request goes to someone else's servers. For legal, health, finance and internal work, that's the whole problem.

✕You can't prove it's any good

The model's a black box you rent. No measurement, no receipt, no way to show it beats a cheaper option.
